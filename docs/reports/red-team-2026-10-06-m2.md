**Total weighted: 4.0 ≥ 3.0 floor.** All MEDIUM/LOW findings fixed before gates.

## Lens results (no findings)

- **Panic sweep:** no `unwrap`/`expect` on external data in `src/`. Hostile settings slugs → `Err(Corrupt)`; hostile wire times → `Err(InvalidData)`; ct proves both.
- **Privacy (KEYLESS-PRIVACY):** no telemetry/logging; `MawaqitClient` constructed keyless with 5 s/15 s timeouts; no disk writes outside the settings path.
- **Layering:** `use mawaqit_api` appears only under `src/infrastructure/` — now machine-checked by `just layering` inside `just verify` (ADR-0002 §6).
- **Honesty:** settings corruption is reported, never swallowed (`Corrupt` variant, ct-tested); month `dropped` days pass through verbatim; slug-less search results fail loud instead of silently shrinking results.
- **TZ-TRUTH:** absent/unparseable mosque tz degrades to `TzSource::Local` — the documented fallback, never a fabricated zone.

## Bach verdict

The lane ships seams, not scaffolding: every port has a production implementation or a named consumer milestone, every claim in the spec EVID is either gate-measured or explicitly pending. The one soft spot is F5 and it is on the books with an owner.
